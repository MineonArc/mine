# MINE on Arc

**Pre-launch · Arc Mainnet · Argus Portal 8**

MINE is designed to direct a share of trading fees from a MINE/USDC launch pool to eligible holders in USDC. The project site explains the proposed fee model and provides an interactive simulator.

[Visit MINE on Arc](https://mineonarc.com/) · [How it works](https://mineonarc.com/how-it-works)

## How the fees are routed

The proposed standard swap fee is **2%** of the trade amount: a 1% MINE tax plus a 1% Argus base fee. Argus Treasury receives **30% of that combined fee**. The remaining 70% is split across the MINE allocations below.

| MINE allocation | Share of the MINE pool |
| --- | ---: |
| Buyback and burn | 43% |
| Liquidity | 38% |
| Holder distributions | 17% |
| Creator | 2% |

With 100,000 USDC in hypothetical daily volume and a constant 0.125% eligible balance share, the simulator estimates a holder credit of **0.30 USDC** for that day, rounded from 0.2975 USDC. The scenario uses the proposed configuration and excludes the launch-window surcharge. Actual credits depend on eligible trades and holder balances.
